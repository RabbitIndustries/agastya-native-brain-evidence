# Independent Evaluator Invitation — Agastya AGI v01

Rabbit Industries Pvt Ltd invites an independent evaluator to verify the provenance of Agastya AGI v01.

## Requested verification scope

The evaluator is asked to independently determine whether the supplied evidence supports:
1. fresh random initialization of the step-0 checkpoint;
2. absence of third-party checkpoint/weight ancestry in the canonical lineage;
3. zero teacher-weight ancestry at step 0;
4. tokenizer identity and provenance;
5. exact unique parameter count;
6. continuous checkpoint lineage from step 0 to the selected trained checkpoint.

A separate benchmark phase may then replay frozen same-suite public evaluations.

## Evidence access

Public evidence:
https://zippri.com/agastya-native-brain-evidence/

Controlled private access can be arranged for proprietary artifacts required for verification, without public redistribution.

## Required output

A signed report with PASS / FAIL / INCONCLUSIVE for each verification gate, artifact hashes, methods, deviations, unresolved questions and report hash.

Rabbit Industries will not describe Agastya as independently verified until the relevant verification gates are passed.
